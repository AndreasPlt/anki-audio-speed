**min**, **max** — slider range, as a factor of normal speed (1.0). Below about 0.6 the voice starts to smear; the pitch stays.

**step** — slider resolution, and how far one key press moves the speed.

**slower_key**, **faster_key**, **normal_key** — reviewer keys. The defaults 8 / 9 / 0 sit next to Anki's own audio keys 5 / 6 / 7 (pause, back, forward).

Each new card starts at 1.0; the answer side keeps the speed set on the question side. Changes to min, max and step show on the slider from the next review session.
